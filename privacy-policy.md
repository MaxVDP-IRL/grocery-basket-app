# Privacy Policy — Basket

**Last updated: April 2026**

## Who we are

Basket is a Chrome extension developed by Max van de Poll (contact: max.vandepoll@gmail.com). It helps users plan weekly meals and fill online grocery baskets.

## What data Basket collects

Basket does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any developer-controlled server. All data the extension handles stays on your device.

The following information is saved locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage:

- Your shopping list (product names, prices, and retailer product IDs)
- Meal plan selections and serving size preferences
- Favourite recipes
- Meal history (which recipes you have added to your list, and when)
- Pantry items you have marked as already in your kitchen
- Cached ingredient price data fetched from the retailer

None of this data leaves your device except as described below.

## How Basket uses your data

When you search for a product or add items to your basket, the extension sends requests directly from your browser to the grocery retailer's website (currently barbora.ee) using your own existing logged-in session. This is equivalent to performing those actions manually on the retailer's website. The developer has no visibility into these requests.

The extension reads one authentication cookie (X-Session-ID) from barbora.ee to confirm you are logged in before making requests. It does not create, modify, or share cookies.

## What Basket does not do

- Does not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information
- Does not track your browsing activity
- Does not run any analytics or advertising code
- Does not send any data to the developer or any third party
- Does not execute code fetched from remote servers

## Third-party services

Basket interacts with barbora.ee on your behalf. Your use of barbora.ee is governed by Barbora's own terms of service and privacy policy. Basket has no affiliation with Barbora or its parent company.

## Data retention and deletion

All data is stored locally in your browser. You can clear it at any time by removing the extension, or by using the clear functions within the extension itself. Uninstalling Basket removes all locally stored data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
